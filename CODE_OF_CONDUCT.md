# Code of Conduct

PDFJiff adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone taking part in the project, in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other channel where you represent the project.

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and kind, especially to newcomers and people whose first language isn't English.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, trolling, doxxing and sustained disruption are not tolerated.
- Respect people's privacy. Never post someone else's personal information, and never share documents that aren't yours to share.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, email the maintainers at **dev@pdfjiff.com**. Include links or screenshots if you can. Every report is reviewed, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential. If the report involves a maintainer, say so, and the report will be handled by someone else.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines, applied in proportion to the harm: a private correction, then a warning, then a temporary ban, then a permanent ban. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this code.
